**Beauty and the Beast**

Beauty and the Beast is a romantic fantasy movie produced by Walt Disney Pictures. It tells the story of a young woman named Belle, who is kind, intelligent, and loves to read books. She becomes a prisoner in a mysterious castle owned by a Beast, who was once a prince cursed by an enchantress. Over time, Belle and the Beast learn to understand and care for each other, proving that true beauty comes from within. The movie features memorable songs such as *Be Our Guest* and *Beauty and the Beast* The story teaches lessons about love, kindness, and looking beyond appearances.
---
**Main Characters**
1. *Belle* – A kind and intelligent young woman who loves to read.
2. *Beast / Prince Adam* – A prince cursed to live as a beast until he learns to love and be loved in return.
3. *Gaston* – A proud and selfish hunter who wants to marry Belle.
